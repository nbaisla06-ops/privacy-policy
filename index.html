Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Store Developer Policies

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sirox (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Sirox mobile application and associated web services.

By downloading, creating an account, or browsing Sirox, you consent to the information practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please uninstall and discontinue using our application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, upload content, interact with creators, or communicate with us:

  • Account & Profile Information: Username, display name, email address, password hash (via secure Firebase Authentication), and profile photo.
  • User-Generated Content: Videos you record or upload, video captions, hashtags, music selections, and comments.
  • Engagement Data: Likes, follows, watch history, views, and interactions within the video feed.
  • Technical & Device Information: Device model, operating system version, anonymous unique device identifiers, and crash logs for stability and performance.

3. Device Permissions We Request

To deliver a full-featured short-video experience, Sirox requests the following device permissions only when necessary:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Required when you choose to record new videos directly inside the Sirox studio camera.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required to capture audio and voice while recording videos.
  • Storage / Photos / Media (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to pick and upload pre-recorded videos and profile photos from your gallery.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to stream videos, synchronize feed algorithms, and communicate securely with our servers.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, personalize, and improve our video recommendation algorithm (serving high-quality content matching your interests).
  • To process video uploads, encode media, and deliver fast video streaming via Cloudflare high-speed CDN.
  • To enable social features such as following creators, liking, commenting, and notifying you about engagement.
  • To maintain security, detect fraud, monitor content violations, and protect user safety.
  • To diagnose application bugs, analyze performance, and deploy software updates.

5. Data Storage, Security & Cloud Infrastructure

All video and media files are stored securely in Cloudflare R2 Object Storage and accelerated through Cloudflare Global Edge Network. Application state and user accounts are managed via encrypted database protocols.

We implement industry-standard cryptographic practices (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) to safeguard your data during transmission and at rest. We never sell your personal data to third parties or data brokers.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full control over your personal data on Sirox. Under Google Play Store user data policies, you have the right to:

  • Access & Edit: Review and update your profile information, avatar, and username at any time in the Profile tab.
  • Delete Content: Permanently delete any video you have uploaded through the in-app options menu.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can permanently request full deletion of your account, profile, uploaded videos, and engagement history by emailing us at supportsirox@gmail.com with the subject line “Request Account Deletion”. Your data will be permanently wiped from our database and storage within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

Sirox is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the “Effective Date” on this page.
