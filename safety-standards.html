Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy Against CSAE & Minor Endangerment

Sirox (Sirox - Short Video App) maintains an absolute, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), minor endangerment, and any content or behavior that harms, exploits, grooms, or endangers minors.

Users are strictly prohibited from uploading, streaming, sharing, linking to, soliciting, or distributing any content that involves or promotes:

  • Any form of CSAE or unlawful exploitation, abuse, or mistreatment of minors.
  • Grooming, coercion, extortion, or inappropriate solicitation directed at minors via videos, comments, profiles, or direct messages.
  • Any depiction, encouragement, or facilitation of dangerous, harmful, or illegal activities involving minors.
  • Accounts, usernames, hashtags, thumbnails, captions, or links designed to promote or circulate prohibited content involving minors.

2. Age Eligibility & Platform Safeguards

Sirox is designed exclusively for users aged 13 years and older (or the minimum digital consent age required in the user’s jurisdiction). We do not knowingly permit children under 13 to create accounts or submit personal data. Accounts suspected of belonging to underage users or engaging in unsafe behavior toward minors are investigated and removed promptly.

3. In-App Reporting & Automated Protection Mechanisms

Sirox provides accessible, built-in safety tools so every user can immediately report harmful videos or offending accounts directly inside the app:

  • In-App Video Reporting: Tap the report / options menu on any short video to select a specific violation reason (including safety, harmful content, harassment, or policy violations) and submit an instant report to our moderation queue.
  • In-App Account & Profile Reporting: Users can report any offending creator profile or account directly from the profile screen so repeat violators can be reviewed and permanently removed.
  • Automated 10-Report Review Hide: When a video receives 10 unique user reports, Sirox automatically sets the video’s status to hidden (under review), immediately removing it from all public feeds and preventing other users from viewing it while our Trust & Safety team conducts a review.
  • Creator Self-Deletion & Transparency: Creators are notified when a video is under review and may permanently delete the video themselves at any time.
  • Direct Safety Email Escalation: Users, parents, guardians, and third parties may also report urgent safety concerns directly by emailing supportsirox@gmail.com.

4. Moderation Review & Enforcement Actions

Our Trust & Safety moderation team actively monitors reported videos and reported accounts through dedicated moderation workflows:

  • Priority Review: All safety reports are reviewed promptly, with urgent minor-safety and CSAE reports prioritized for immediate action within 24 hours (and standard reports reviewed within 24–48 hours).
  • Immediate Content Purge: Confirmed violating videos and associated media files are permanently deleted from our Cloudflare D1 database and Cloudflare R2 storage.
  • Permanent Account Termination & Ban: Accounts that violate our CSAE or minor safety standards face immediate, permanent account deletion and platform bans, blocking further access and re-use of the account.
  • Copyright & Policy Remedies: Where applicable for non-safety copyright claims, our team can remotely mute infringing audio or remove non-compliant content.

5. Legal Compliance & Reporting to Authorities

Sirox is committed to full compliance with all applicable local, national, and international child protection laws and regulations, including COPPA, applicable IT and digital safety rules, and Google Play’s Safety Standards policy.

Whenever confirmed CSAE or unlawful minor-endangerment content is identified on Sirox, we take immediate action to remove the content, permanently ban the offending account, preserve relevant account and incident metadata as required by law, and report confirmed cases to the appropriate national and regional law enforcement authorities and authorized reporting organizations (such as NCMEC / CyberTipline and national cybercrime reporting authorities).
